# Amidah — version anglaise à relire

> **Pour Dr Justin (en français).** Version anglaise préparée le 28 octobre 2026 à ta demande (« Traduis quand même »). Elle suit, passage par passage, les 61 passages du texte français affiché sur le site (source : PDF hassidout.org). Elle est rédigée d'après le sens de la prière traditionnelle, sans reprendre mot à mot une traduction anglaise publiée.
>
> - « D.ieu » est rendu par « G-d », la même convention en anglais.
> - Les gestes et indications (genoux, fêtes, dix jours de pénitence) sont traduits comme en français.
> - Les droits du texte français de hassidout.org restent à confirmer, comme noté le 25/10.
>

## Passage 1 — Geste

Take three steps back, then three steps forward, and say:

## Passage 2 — Prière

My Lord, open my lips, and my mouth shall declare Your praise.

## Passage 3 — Geste

Bend the knees at “Blessed”; stand upright at “Lord our G-d”:

## Passage 4 — Prière

Blessed are You, Lord our G-d and G-d of our fathers, G-d of Abraham, G-d of Isaac and G-d of Jacob, the great, mighty and awesome G-d, G-d Most High, who bestows abundant kindness, who creates all things, who remembers the devotion of the patriarchs, and who in love brings a redeemer to their children’s children, for the sake of His Name.

## Passage 5 — Indication

During the Ten Days of Repentance, add:

## Passage 6 — Prière

Remember us for life, King who desires life; inscribe us in the Book of Life, for Your sake, living G-d.

## Passage 7 — Geste

Bend the knees at “Blessed”; stand upright at “Lord”:

## Passage 8 — Prière

King, Helper, Savior and Shield. Blessed are You, Lord, Shield of Abraham.

## Passage 9 — Prière

You are mighty forever, my Lord; You give life to the dead; You are great to save.

## Passage 10 — Indication

In summer, say: “He causes the dew to fall.”

## Passage 11 — Indication

In winter, say: “He causes the wind to blow and the rain to fall.”

## Passage 12 — Prière

He sustains the living with kindness, gives life to the dead with great mercy, supports those who fall, heals the sick, frees the bound, and keeps faith with those who sleep in the dust. Who is like You, Master of mighty deeds! And who can be compared to You, King, who brings death and restores life, and causes salvation to spring forth!

## Passage 13 — Indication

During the Ten Days of Repentance, add:

## Passage 14 — Prière

Who is like You, merciful Father, who in compassion remembers His creatures for life.

## Passage 15 — Prière

You are faithful to give life to the dead. Blessed are You, Lord, who gives life to the dead.

## Passage 16 — Indication

When the Chazzan repeats the Amidah, the Kedushah is said here.

## Passage 17 — Prière

We will sanctify and revere You with the sweet words of the assembly of holy seraphim, who proclaim You “holy” three times, as it is written by Your prophet: And they call to one another and say: (Congregation and Chazzan:) “Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory.” (Chazzan:) Those facing them give praise and say: (Congregation and Chazzan:) “Blessed be the glory of the Lord from His place.” (Chazzan:) And in Your holy Scriptures it is written: (Congregation and Chazzan:) “The Lord shall reign forever; your G-d, O Zion, for all generations. Praise the Lord.”

## Passage 18 — Prière

You are holy and Your Name is holy, and holy beings praise You every day, forever. Blessed are You, Lord, the holy G-d. (During the Ten Days of Repentance, say instead: the holy King.)

## Passage 19 — Prière

You graciously give knowledge to man and teach understanding to mortals. Graciously grant us from You wisdom, understanding and knowledge. Blessed are You, Lord, who graciously gives knowledge.

## Passage 20 — Prière

Bring us back, our Father, to Your Torah; draw us near, our King, to Your service; and return us to You in complete repentance. Blessed are You, Lord, who desires repentance.

## Passage 21 — Prière

Forgive us, our Father, for we have sinned; pardon us, our King, for we have transgressed; for You are a good and forgiving G-d. Blessed are You, Lord, gracious One who forgives abundantly.

## Passage 22 — Prière

See our affliction and plead our cause; redeem us speedily for the sake of Your Name, for You, G-d, are the mighty Redeemer. Blessed are You, Lord, Redeemer of Israel.

## Passage 23 — Prière

Heal us, Lord, and we shall be healed; save us and we shall be saved; for You are our praise. Grant complete healing for all our wounds; for You, Almighty King, are a faithful and merciful healer. Blessed are You, Lord, who heals the sick of His people Israel.

## Passage 24 — Prière

Bless for us, Lord our G-d, this year and all its kinds of produce for good; and grant (in the summer season, say:) blessing (in the winter season, say: dew and rain for blessing) upon the face of the earth. Satisfy us with Your goodness and bless our year like the good years, for blessing; for You are a generous G-d who does good and blesses the years. Blessed are You, Lord, who blesses the years.

## Passage 25 — Prière

Sound the great shofar for our freedom; raise a banner to gather our exiles, and gather us together from the four corners of the earth into our land. Blessed are You, Lord, who gathers the dispersed of His people Israel.

## Passage 26 — Prière

Restore our judges as at first, and our counselors as at the beginning; remove from us sorrow and sighing, and reign over us, You alone, Lord, with kindness and compassion, with righteousness and justice. Blessed are You, Lord, King who loves righteousness and justice. (During the Ten Days of Repentance, say instead: the King of judgment.)

## Passage 27 — Prière

Let there be no hope for informers, and may all heretics and all the wicked perish in an instant; may all the enemies of Your people be swiftly cut off; and may You speedily uproot, break, crush and humble the reign of wickedness, quickly, in our days. Blessed are You, Lord, who breaks enemies and humbles the wicked.

## Passage 28 — Prière

May Your compassion be stirred, Lord our G-d, toward the righteous, toward the pious, toward the elders of Your people, the House of Israel, toward the remnant of their sages, toward the righteous converts, and toward us. Grant a good reward to all who truly trust in Your Name, and set our portion among them; may we never be put to shame, for we have put our trust in You. Blessed are You, Lord, support and security of the righteous.

## Passage 29 — Prière

Return in mercy to Jerusalem, Your city, and dwell in it as You have promised; establish in it soon the throne of David, Your servant, and rebuild it soon, in our days, as an everlasting building. Blessed are You, Lord, who rebuilds Jerusalem.

## Passage 30 — Prière

Cause the offspring of David, Your servant, to flourish speedily, and raise up his strength through Your salvation, for we hope for Your salvation all day long. Blessed are You, Lord, who causes the strength of salvation to flourish.

## Passage 31 — Prière

Hear our voice, Lord our G-d; merciful Father, have compassion on us and accept our prayers in mercy and favor, for You are G-d who hears prayers and supplications; do not turn us away from You, our King, empty-handed, for You hear the prayer of everyone. Blessed are You, Lord, who hears prayer.

## Passage 32 — Prière

Look with favor, Lord our G-d, on Your people Israel and be attentive to their prayer; restore the service to Your Sanctuary, and accept with love and favor the fire-offerings and the prayer of Israel; and may the service of Your people Israel always be pleasing to You.

## Passage 33 — Indication

On Rosh Chodesh and Chol HaMoed, “Our G-d … may there rise” is said here.

## Passage 34 — Prière

Our G-d and G-d of our fathers, may there rise, come and reach, be seen, accepted and heard, be recalled and remembered before You, the remembrance of us, the remembrance of our fathers, the remembrance of Mashiach, son of David, Your servant, the remembrance of Jerusalem, Your holy city, and the remembrance of all Your people, the House of Israel, for deliverance, for well-being, for grace, kindness and mercy, for good life and for peace, on this day of

## Passage 35 — Indication

On Rosh Chodesh: Rosh Chodesh

## Passage 36 — Indication

On the festival of Pesach: the Festival of Matzot

## Passage 37 — Indication

On the festival of Sukkot: the Festival of Sukkot.

## Passage 38 — Prière

Remember us on this [day], Lord our G-d, for good; recall us on this [day] for blessing; save us on this [day] for good life. With a word of salvation and compassion, spare us and be gracious to us; have mercy on us and save us; for our eyes are turned to You, for You, G-d, are a gracious and merciful King.

## Passage 39 — Prière

May our eyes behold Your return to Zion in mercy. Blessed are You, Lord, who restores His Divine Presence to Zion.

## Passage 40 — Geste

Bend the knees at “We thankfully acknowledge”; stand upright before “Lord our G-d”:

## Passage 41 — Prière

We thankfully acknowledge that You are the Lord our G-d and G-d of our fathers forever. You are the Rock of our life, the Shield of our salvation in every generation. We will give thanks to You and tell Your praise, evening, morning and noon, for our lives entrusted into Your hand, for our souls placed in Your care, for Your miracles that are with us every day, and for Your continual wonders and goodness. You are the Good One, for Your mercies never cease; the Merciful One, for Your kindnesses never end; for we have always put our hope in You.

## Passage 42 — Indication

On Chanukah and Purim, the following is added.

## Passage 43 — Prière

And [we thank You] for the miracles, for the redemption, for the mighty deeds, for the saving acts and for the wonders You performed for our ancestors in those days, at this season —

## Passage 44 — Indication

On Chanukah, continue here:

## Passage 45 — Prière

In the days of Mattityahu, son of Yochanan the High Priest, the Hasmonean, and his sons, when the wicked Hellenic government rose up against Your people Israel to make them forget Your Torah and transgress the decrees of Your will. But You, in Your abundant mercy, stood by them in the time of their distress. You fought their battles, defended their rights and avenged the wrong done to them. You delivered the mighty into the hands of the weak, the many into the hands of the few, the impure into the hands of the pure, the wicked into the hands of the righteous, and the arrogant into the hands of those who occupy themselves with Your Torah. You made for Yourself a great and holy Name in Your world, and for Your people You brought about a great deliverance and redemption to this very day. Then Your children came into the sanctuary of Your House, cleansed Your Temple, purified Your Sanctuary, kindled lights in Your holy courts, and established these eight days of Chanukah to give thanks and praise to Your great Name.

## Passage 46 — Indication

On Purim, continue here:

## Passage 47 — Prière

In the days of Mordechai and Esther, in Shushan the capital, when the wicked Haman rose up against them and sought to destroy, slay and wipe out all the Jews, young and old, children and women, on a single day, the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, the month of Adar, and to take their spoil as plunder. But You, in Your abundant mercy, frustrated his counsel and thwarted his intention, and turned back upon his own head the evil he had planned; and they hanged him and his sons on the gallows.

## Passage 48 — Prière

And for all these things, may Your Name, our King, be blessed and exalted continually, forever and ever.

## Passage 49 — Indication

During the Ten Days of Repentance, add:

## Passage 50 — Prière

Inscribe all the children of Your covenant for a good life.

## Passage 51 — Prière

And all living things shall thank You forever and praise Your great Name for all time, for You are good. G-d, You are our everlasting salvation and help, O good G-d.

## Passage 52 — Geste

Bend the knees at “Blessed”; stand upright at “Lord our G-d”:

## Passage 53 — Prière

Blessed are You, Lord, the Good One is Your Name, and to You it is fitting to give thanks.

## Passage 54 — Prière

Grant peace, goodness and blessing, life, grace, kindness and mercy, to us and to all Your people Israel. Bless us, our Father, all of us as one, with the light of Your countenance. For by the light of Your countenance You have given us, Lord our G-d, the Torah of life and loving-kindness, righteousness, blessing, mercy, life and peace. May it be good in Your eyes to bless Your people Israel at every time and at every hour with Your peace.

## Passage 55 — Indication

During the Ten Days of Repentance, add:

## Passage 56 — Prière

And in the book of life, blessing, peace and prosperity, deliverance, consolation and favorable decrees, may we and all Your people, the House of Israel, be remembered and inscribed before You for a good life and for peace.

## Passage 57 — Prière

Blessed are You, Lord, who blesses His people Israel with peace. May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable before You, Lord, my Rock and my Redeemer.

## Passage 58 — Prière

My G-d, guard my tongue from evil and my lips from speaking deceit. To those who curse me, let my soul be silent; let my soul be as dust to all. Open my heart to Your Torah, and let my soul eagerly pursue Your commandments. As for all who plot evil against me, swiftly annul their counsel and frustrate their design. Let them be like chaff before the wind; let the angel of the Lord drive them away. That Your beloved ones may be delivered, help with Your right hand and answer me. Do it for the sake of Your Name; do it for the sake of Your right hand; do it for the sake of Your Torah; do it for the sake of Your holiness. May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable before You, Lord, my Rock and my Redeemer.

## Passage 59 — Geste

Before saying the following verses, bow slightly and, while bowing, take three steps back. While bowing, turn the head to the left and say: “He who makes peace in His heights”; bow forward and say: “may He make”; turn the head to the right and say: “peace for us”; and finally bow forward and say: “and for all Israel; and say: Amen.”

## Passage 60 — Prière

May it be Your will, Lord our G-d and G-d of our fathers, that the Holy Temple be speedily rebuilt in our days, and grant us our portion in Your Torah.

## Passage 61 — Fin

End of the Amidah
